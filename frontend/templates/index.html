Understanding the Records

Estate / Tenancy Records
1841–1886

Estate and tenancy records document the relationship between landlords and tenants across the Fitzwilliam estate in County Wicklow. These records include tenant lists showing the head of household, townland location, landholding size, and rental terms.

Source: Fitzwilliam Papers
Key Details: Names, townlands, land size, rent amounts
Eviction Records
1845–1860

Eviction records (also called Ejectment Books) document tenants who failed to pay their rent and faced the threat of losing their homes. These records include Notices to Quit, court proceedings at Tinahely Court House, and details of arrears owed.

Source: Fitzwilliam Papers MS 4972
Key Details: Arrears amounts, court dates, outcomes
Emigration Records
1847–1856

Emigration records document the assisted migration scheme operated by the Fitzwilliam estate during and after the Great Famine. These records include names, ages, family relationships, ship names, departure dates, and arrival ports in Canada.

Source: Fitzwilliam Papers MS 4974-5
Key Details: Ship names, departure/arrival dates, destinations
Workhouse Records
1840s–1900s

Workhouse records document individuals and families who sought refuge in the local workhouse (the "Poor House") during times of extreme poverty, particularly during and after the Great Famine. These records include admissions, births, deaths, and discharges from the workhouse.

Source: National Archives, Workhouse Registers
Key Details: Admission dates, family connections, outcomes

Together, these records provide a comprehensive picture of life in County Wicklow during one of Ireland's most turbulent periods.

FAQ

Common questions about the archive.

Why use townlands?

Townlands are stable geographic units used consistently across Irish historical records, enabling linkage and map-based exploration.

What surnames appear in the dropdown?

Suggestions come directly from the unified records currently in scope. If a townland is selected, only surnames present in that townland are shown.
