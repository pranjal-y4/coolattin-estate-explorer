Featured Family Records

Real families, real histories drawn from the original Fitzwilliam estate papers.

Byrne Family · Coolboy
Tenancy · Eviction · Emigration

The Byrnes of Coolboy are one of the most thoroughly documented families in the Fitzwilliam archive, with 51 records spanning tenancy surveys, ejectment proceedings, and assisted emigration lists between 1845 and 1868.

Townland: Coolboy · Barony of Shillelagh
Period: 1845–1868 · 51 records
Sources: Fitzwilliam Papers MS 4970 · MS 4972 · MS 4974-5
Fox Family · Coolboy
Tenancy · Eviction · Emigration

The Fox family of Coolboy appear across tenancy surveys, Ejectment Books recording arrears and Notices to Quit, and the assisted emigration lists, covering 43 records tracing displacement and departure during the post-Famine clearances of 1845–1852.

Townland: Coolboy · Barony of Shillelagh
Period: 1845–1852 · 43 records
Sources: Fitzwilliam Papers MS 4972 · MS 4974-5
Healy Family · Killinure
Tenancy · Eviction · Emigration

Members of the Healy family of Killinure are recorded across 52 entries spanning tenancy, eviction, and Fitzwilliam-assisted emigration. Ships departed from Liverpool with arrivals at Quebec and New York between 1847 and 1851.

Townland: Killinure · Barony of Shillelagh
Period: 1847–1886 · 52 records
Sources: Fitzwilliam Papers MS 4974-5 · MS 4970

FAQ

Common questions about the archive.

Why use townlands?

Townlands are stable geographic units used consistently across Irish historical records, enabling linkage and map-based exploration.

What surnames appear in the dropdown?

Suggestions come directly from the unified records currently in scope. If a townland is selected, only surnames present in that townland are shown.
